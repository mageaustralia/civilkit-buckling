# Trademarks

CivilKit Buckling's code is open source under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). You may use it,
change it, fork it and host it, for any purpose, under that licence's terms.

The licence covers the code, not the brand. The names **CivilKit** and **CivilKit Buckling**, the
CivilKit logo and the app's icons (`icons/`) identify the CivilKit tools by Mage Engineering, and
the Apache licence does not grant rights to them (its section 6 says so). This note says how you can use them.

## You may, without asking

- **Say what your work is built on.** A fork, a hosted copy or a product that uses this code may
  say it is "based on CivilKit Buckling", with a link to
  <https://github.com/mageaustralia/civilkit-buckling> or <https://mageengineering.com.au>.
- **Mirror it unmodified.** A copy that is the unchanged code, kept up to date or pinned to a
  release, may keep the name, the logo and the icons.
- **Talk about it.** Write about CivilKit Buckling, teach with it, review it and link to it, by
  name.

## Please don't

- Ship a **modified** version, hosted or packaged, under the name CivilKit or CivilKit Buckling,
  or with the CivilKit logo or the app's icons. Give it your own name and icon, change the header
  link and the footer, and say "based on CivilKit Buckling".
- Use the names or the logo in a way that suggests Mage Engineering made, reviewed or endorses
  your version, your service or your results.
- Use "CivilKit" in the name of your own product, domain or package.

Modifying the code is fine: that is what the licence is for. The rule is only that a changed copy
carries its own name, so nobody mistakes it, or its numbers, for ours.
